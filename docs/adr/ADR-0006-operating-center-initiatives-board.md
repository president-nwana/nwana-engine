# ADR 0006 Operating Center Initiatives and Board Workflow

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-09-19

## Context

NWANA Engine was initially described mainly as a way to create or connect an object, choose a required result, and launch distribution work. That remains necessary but is not sufficient for normal organizational operation.

The owner also receives unstructured thoughts, dormant plans, Word documents, spreadsheets, contact files, opportunities, problems, and proposals whose useful outcome is not known in advance. NWANA Board work creates additional questions, decisions, assignments, and follow-up. A static action screen would leave those inputs outside the machine and force the organization to depend on chat memory or manual reconstruction.

Board meetings normally occur weekly, but the project prohibits recurring polling and timers. Board members need to contribute agenda material whenever it arises, and meeting preparation must read those stored submissions when requested.

## Decision

NWANA Engine will provide a connected operating center with six work areas:

1. Overview;
2. Actions;
3. Initiatives and Source Materials;
4. Board;
5. Decisions and Approvals;
6. Results and History.

### Overview

Overview presents verified current state across competitions and calendar items, results, registrations, sponsorship, donations, people and partner pipelines, channel outcomes, errors, blockers, approvals, and required decisions. Empty or unavailable data remains explicit rather than being invented.

### Initiative intake

Engine accepts a thought, problem, opportunity, task, document, spreadsheet, contact file, or other source material without requiring the submitter to know the final object or workflow.

For each intake, Engine:

- interprets the intended outcome and affected NWANA objects;
- separates verified facts from unsupported, outdated, conflicting, or missing information;
- identifies missing organizations or contacts;
- proposes specific results, audiences, offers, channels, actions, decisions, and follow-up;
- uses an existing verified mechanism when available;
- asks for owner judgment when alternatives materially differ;
- routes strategic, governance, legal, costly, unsupported, or otherwise consequential work to the owner or Board;
- converts an approved proposal into tracked work.

### Board submissions and agenda

Every authorized Board member may submit any number of agenda items on any day. An item may be a question, proposal, report, requested decision, discussion item, supporting document, or request to speak.

Each item preserves at least:

- author;
- submission time;
- requested meeting when supplied;
- item type;
- title and description;
- supporting material;
- requested outcome;
- status.

Opening a meeting displays all pending submissions and the machine-prepared summary from verified records. No recurring timer or polling is required.

### Live meeting record

During a meeting, Engine records attendance, agenda items, discussion, motions or proposals, decisions, votes when applicable, responsible person, due date, and follow-up.

A confirmed decision immediately becomes one or more linked work items. Engine may execute an action only when the meeting decision and existing system policy authorize it and the required integration is verified. Otherwise Engine creates the exact approval, dependency, or blocker.

After the meeting, Engine produces minutes, preserves the decision record, tracks assigned work, and carries unresolved items into the next requested briefing.

## Consequences

- Ideas and files no longer remain disconnected from operating work.
- Board decisions become traceable to execution and outcomes.
- The owner can see both current state and required action in one product.
- Board-member submissions do not depend on the president manually assembling the agenda.
- The operating center requires authorization boundaries for Board members and must preserve authorship and decision history.
- This decision adds no timer, cron, polling process, paid service, or automatic expenditure.
